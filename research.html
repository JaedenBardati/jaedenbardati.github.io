Research

My primary research interests are in supermassive black holes, galaxies and gravitational waves. I have recently been particularly interested in characterizing the structure and dynamics of unresolved regions of active galactic nuclei using highly multiscale zoom-in radiation-magnetohydrodynamics simulations. My home page lists several of my recent projects.

Check out my links for NASA/ADS, INSPIRE-HEP, Google Scholar, or ORCID for a full list of publications.
